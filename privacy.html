Privacy Policy - EWishare - UK GDPR Compliant

EWishare = Everything Within WiFi - No cloud storage. All files transfer directly via WiFi P2P or WebRTC, not stored on our servers. We do not collect personal data, no tracking, only essential PWA cache.

What we store

  • 8-digit PIN temporarily in memory, expires 5 min
  • Essential service worker cache for offline PWA
  • No personal files stored on server

UK Law Compliance

Compliant with UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018. No data transferred outside UK/EEA except via user's own WiFi. No cookies except essential. Right to erasure: Exit button clears all cache via localStorage.clear(), sessionStorage.clear(), caches.delete().

For data requests: privacy@ewishare.example - UK based.
